About

I'm a second-year PhD student at the University of Texas at Austin, where I work with Dr. Keri Stephens at the Technology and Information Policy Institute. My research asks how AI gets its meaning: in the stories employees tell about their jobs, in what the public says online about new technologies, and in the choices organizations make when they adopt them.

I also study disaster and risk communication. I'm part of teams working on how people decide whether to evacuate during flash floods, and on a communication toolkit that helps Texas floodplain managers talk about flood risk with their communities.

Alongside the PhD, I'm earning an M.Ed. in Quantitative Methods, which gives me a strong statistics foundation for this work. Before Austin, I earned an M.A. in Organizational Communication at Baylor University and a B.Sc. in Mass Communication at the University of Lagos. This fall I'm teaching CMS 318L, Strategic and Risk Communication.
